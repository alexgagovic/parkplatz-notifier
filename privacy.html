We are the controller for the personal data described here, within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short

Parkplatz Notifier helps a group of people share a car park. To do that, the app needs to know who you are, which group you belong to, and whether you are currently using a space — and it needs to show that to the other members of your group.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Your location is never uploaded to us.

What we store, and why

Your account

Your email address, and — if you choose to provide them — your name, username, display name, phone number and profile picture. The email address is needed to create the account and sign you in. The rest exists so that the other members of your group can recognise you, rather than seeing an email address.

Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.

Legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Your parking activity

Whether you currently occupy a space, which group that space is in, whether you have marked yourself as not needing a space today, and your weekly schedule of which days you need a space and when you expect to arrive. This is the core function of the app and is visible to the other members of your group.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Your groups

Which parking groups you belong to, and whether you administer them. A group record holds the name and address of the car park and the number of spaces it has.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Messages

Messages you send in a group chat are stored and shown to the other members of that group.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Invitations

When a member invites someone, we store the invited email address so that the invitation can be delivered when that person signs up.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in making group invitations work.

Device token for notifications

If you allow notifications, we store the token that Apple issues to your device, so that we can deliver them.

Legal basis: your consent, given at the iOS prompt and withdrawable at any time in iOS Settings, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Security data

If you set an administrator PIN, we store it only as a cryptographic hash — never the PIN itself. We also store a count of failed attempts and any resulting lockout time, to prevent guessing.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in securing the service.

Location

If you allow location access, the app sets up a geofence around your group's parking address, so that it can offer you a space when you arrive.

Your location is not transmitted to us and is not stored on our servers. The comparison between your position and the parking address happens entirely on your device. We never receive coordinates describing where you are.

Location access is optional. Every feature except the arrival reminder works without it, and you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings. Booking and releasing a space do not require location access and are not restricted by where you are.

Who can see your data

Other members of your groups can see your name, profile picture, whether you are using a space, your weekly schedule and your messages. That is the point of the app: a shared car park only works if the people sharing it can see who is using it. People outside your groups cannot see this.

Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, as our processor, under the Firebase platform: Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and Analytics. Data is processed on Google's infrastructure, which may involve transfers to the United States. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on Standard Contractual Clauses. Google's terms are available at firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms.

Apple, for delivering push notifications to your device.

We do not otherwise disclose your data, except where we are legally obliged to.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used — which screens are opened, how often groups are created — so that we can improve it. This is not used to build advertising profiles, and we do not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps or services.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in improving the service.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account it is removed as described below. Backups are overwritten in the normal course of operation.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Edit Profile → Delete Account. There is no need to email us and nothing to wait for.

This deletes your account and sign-in credentials, your profile and profile picture, your parking status and schedule, your notifications and invitations, and any parking groups you created — including those groups' chat history. The other members of a group you created are notified that it has closed.

Messages you sent in groups created by other people are kept, because they form part of a conversation belonging to those people. If you would like those removed as well, write to us at the address above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Write to alexgagovic.srb@gmail.com to exercise any of these. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority; in Austria that is the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).

Children

The app is not directed at children. In Austria, consent to information society services can be given from the age of 14. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is restricted by server-side security rules, so that group data is readable only by the members of that group, and account data only by its owner. Administrator PINs are stored only as hashes.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
